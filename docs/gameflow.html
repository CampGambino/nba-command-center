Stints are reconstructed from the play-by-play substitution log. Period-opening lineups aren't published, so they're inferred from in-period activity and reconciled against the box score — the badge by the score names the source and reports the match.

One source: ESPN's play-by-play, timestamped to the second. This season's finished games are read once by the Command Center's nightly build and kept on this site; anything else (older seasons, tonight's games) is read from ESPN when you open it. ESPN publishes whole minutes in its box score, so reconstructed floor time is checked to within half a minute. The minutes, points and +/− columns come straight from the box score.

Ported from NBA GameFlow 6, which was built first for the WNBA. Its agreement figures were measured on WNBA games; they should be re-measured against NBA games before being trusted to that precision. Inspired by, and not affiliated with, PopcornMachine.net.
