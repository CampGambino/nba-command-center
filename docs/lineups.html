NBA.com's hustle stats, which ESPN doesn't carry: shots contested (inside and outside the arc), deflections, charges drawn, screen assists (a screen that leads straight to a basket, and the points from them), loose balls recovered and box-outs.
From NBA.com's rotation data: every check-in and check-out to the tenth of a second, so minutes are exact. Points for and against come from the play-by-play's score at those moments; a point scored at the very moment of a substitution counts for the five who were on before it, which can put a free throw on the wrong side of a substitution (plus-minus matches ESPN's box score for 97% of player-games, off by 1 point in the rest). "Per 48" scales a lineup's margin to a full game; a small number of minutes says little.
