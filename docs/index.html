      History in tonight's notes

      Lines from each team's game notes, word for word as the team's PR staff wrote them: history made or in reach, league ranks, "one of N" clubs, active streaks, races on a list, and the series. The tag says what kind of fact it is; the line under it is what tonight can change (bold = in play tonight), from the table printed beside it in the notes or this season's box scores. New = not in the team's earlier notes. A player is named only when the sentence names him. Trimmed (listed under each team): closed past one-offs nothing tonight can add to (a season-opener total, a pass made weeks ago) unless rare over 10+ years, filler, a home-only stat on a road night, and lines the page layout garbled; a team with few lines keeps its best ones.
