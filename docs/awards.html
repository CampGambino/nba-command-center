The 65-game rule (CBA Article XXIX, Section 6) MVP, Defensive Player of the Year, Most Improved, All-NBA and All-Defensive need:
  • 65 games, where a game counts at 20 minutes. Only two games a season count at 15 to 19:59. Anything under that doesn't count.
  • Or 62 games plus a season-ending injury (a doctor picked by the league and the union says he likely can't play through May 31) and 85% of his team's games before it.
  • Once 65 is out of reach: a grievance (his team held him down on purpose; only when an award would raise his max salary or allow a supermax; filed within 2 days) or an extraordinary circumstances challenge (filed at the end of the season). Never both.
Not covered: Rookie of the Year, Sixth Man, Clutch Player, Coach of the Year. "Can miss" = games left for his team minus games he still needs; a game under 20 minutes (after his two 15-minute games are used) is a missed game too.
